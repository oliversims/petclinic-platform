Budget (root, not a module)
===========================

File: terraform/environments/prod/budget.tf

Role: a monthly cost ceiling so the bill does not surprise anyone.
It does not stop spend by itself. It only emails.


What is created

  - Cost budget petclinic-prod-monthly
    Limit: from terraform.tfvars
    Filter: tag Environment = prod
    Emails at 50%, 80%, and 100% of actual spend


How it is used

  AWS sends a confirmation to the address in terraform.tfvars.
  Alerts do not start until that mail is accepted.
  Created on the first apply; does not depend on the cluster.
